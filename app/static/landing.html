The problem

Generic AI chatbots are good with words.
They're a liability with numbers.

HR and Finance teams answer the same questions every day. A chatbot that sounds confident and gets a payout wrong creates more tickets than it closes.

Made-up numbers

"You have 14 days left." Based on what? Language models predict text; they don't run your accrual rules.

No sources

When an answer can't be traced to a policy section, nobody can check it, and HR ends up re-answering it.

Answers outside policy

Ask about something your handbook doesn't cover and a generic bot will still answer. That's how liabilities start.

How it works

Two engines. Each question goes to the right one.

Some answers can be generated. Some have to be guaranteed. Policy Copilot knows the difference.

  • Exact result with every step shown
  • Asks for missing details instead of assuming

Built for HR operations

Everything a policy desk needs, nothing it has to babysit

  • Knowledge base you control. Upload or replace a policy and it's re-indexed in seconds; unchanged files are skipped.
  • Calculations HR can verify. Leave balance, notice period, overtime, expense reimbursement and remote-work eligibility out of the box; your own rules added in days.
  • Audit log. Every question, how it was answered, which sources and rule version were used, and how long it took.
  • API first. The same answers in Slack, Teams, your intranet or your HR portal through a documented REST API.

Security & control

Your documents stay in your infrastructure

  • Self-hosted. Ships as a Docker image with PostgreSQL. Run it in your AWS, GCP or Azure account.
  • Your choice of AI provider. OpenAI or Anthropic Claude, or a fully offline mode for sensitive pilots.
  • Admin-only document changes. Uploads and deletions require an API key.
  • Traceable by design. Answers carry their sources and rule version, and every exchange is logged.
