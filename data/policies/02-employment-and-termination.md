# Employment Terms and Termination

Policy owner: People Operations and Legal. Version 2.4, effective 1 January 2025.

## Probation Period

All new hires serve a probation period of 3 months. For roles at manager level and above, the probation period is 6 months. Probation may be extended once, by up to the same duration, with written justification from the manager and approval from People Operations. During probation, either party may end the employment with 2 weeks of notice.

## Notice Periods

### Standard notice

After probation, the notice period depends on the employee's level and length of continuous service:

| Level | Less than 2 years | 2 to 5 years | More than 5 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual contributor | 1 month | 2 months | 3 months |
| Manager | 2 months | 2 months | 3 months |
| Executive | 3 months | 3 months | 6 months |

The same notice period applies whether the employee resigns or the company ends the employment, unless the employment contract states a longer period, in which case the contract prevails.

### Garden leave and payment in lieu

The company may place an employee on garden leave for all or part of the notice period, or pay base salary in lieu of notice. Payment in lieu of notice is calculated on base salary only and excludes bonuses and equity vesting.

## Resignation

Resignations must be submitted in writing (email to the manager with People Operations in copy is sufficient). The notice period starts the day after the resignation is received. Unused annual leave is paid out on the final payslip, up to the amount accrued in the current year plus any valid carryover.

## Dismissal

Before any dismissal for performance reasons, the employee must have received a documented performance improvement plan (PIP) lasting at least 6 weeks. Dismissal for gross misconduct (for example theft, violence, harassment, or a serious data breach caused intentionally) can take effect immediately without notice, after an investigation and a hearing at which the employee may be accompanied.

## Severance

Employees whose role is eliminated in a restructuring receive severance equal to 2 weeks of base salary per full year of service, with a minimum of 4 weeks and a maximum of 26 weeks. Severance is in addition to the notice period and is conditional on signing a settlement agreement.

## Non-compete and Confidentiality

Confidentiality obligations continue indefinitely after employment ends. A non-compete clause applies only if it is explicitly included in the individual employment contract, and is limited to 12 months and to direct competitors listed in the contract.
