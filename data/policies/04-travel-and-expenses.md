# Travel and Expense Policy

Policy owner: Finance. Version 4.0, effective 1 January 2025. All amounts are in USD or the equivalent in local currency at the exchange rate on the date of the expense.

## General Principles

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses incurred on behalf of Helios Robotics. Expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of being incurred. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense of 25 USD or more. Expenses below 25 USD may be claimed without a receipt, up to a total of 100 USD of receipt-less expenses per month.

## Pre-approval

Any single expense above 1,000 USD requires written pre-approval from the employee's manager before it is incurred. All international travel requires pre-approval regardless of cost.

## Daily Limits by Category

Cities are grouped into two tiers. Tier 1 cities are high-cost locations: New York, San Francisco, London, Paris, Zurich, Tokyo, Singapore. All other cities are Tier 2.

| Category | Tier 1 limit | Tier 2 limit |
|---|---|---|
| Meals (per day) | 75 USD | 50 USD |
| Hotel (per night) | 300 USD | 180 USD |
| Ground transport (per day) | 80 USD | 50 USD |

Amounts above the daily limit are not reimbursed unless they were pre-approved in writing by the manager.

## Flights and Trains

Economy class is the default for all flights. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours. Business class requires approval from a member of the executive team. Train travel is preferred over flying for journeys under 4 hours.

## Client Entertainment

Client meals and entertainment are limited to 120 USD per attendee, including the employee. The names and companies of all attendees must be listed in the expense report. Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of client entertainment.

## Non-reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursed: personal entertainment, fines and traffic tickets, minibar charges, upgrades not approved in advance, travel insurance already covered by the company policy, and expenses for spouses or family members.
