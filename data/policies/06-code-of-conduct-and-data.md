# Code of Conduct and Data Protection

Policy owner: Legal and Compliance. Version 5.0, effective 1 January 2025.

## Respect and Anti-harassment

Helios Robotics does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind, whether based on gender, ethnicity, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. This applies at work, at company events, and in online communication channels.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns can be raised with a manager, with People Operations, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline, which is operated by an independent third party and available 24 hours a day. Every report is acknowledged within 5 working days. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence.

## Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from business partners with a value of up to 50 USD. Gifts above 50 USD must be declared to Compliance within 5 working days and may need to be returned. Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers) must never be accepted, whatever the amount.

## Conflicts of Interest

Any personal relationship, investment, or outside activity that could influence business decisions must be declared to Compliance. Side jobs are allowed if they do not compete with Helios Robotics, do not use company resources, and are declared in advance.

## Data Protection

Personal data of customers and employees may only be processed for the purpose for which it was collected. Access to personal data is granted on a need-to-know basis. Personal data must never be copied to personal devices or uploaded to unapproved AI tools or cloud services.

## Data Breaches

Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@helios-robotics.example within 24 hours of discovery. The company notifies the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required by law.

## Data Retention

Employee records are kept for 6 years after the end of employment. Recruitment data of unsuccessful candidates is deleted after 12 months unless the candidate consents to longer retention.
