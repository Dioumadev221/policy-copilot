# Working Time and Overtime

Policy owner: People Operations and Finance. Version 1.7, effective 1 January 2025.

## Standard Working Hours

The standard working week is 40 hours, normally Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside core hours, employees organise their schedule with their manager.

## Overtime Eligibility

Overtime pay applies only to non-exempt employees, meaning employees paid on an hourly basis. Exempt employees (salaried roles, including all managers and executives) are not paid overtime; their workload is managed through time off in lieu agreed with their manager.

All overtime must be approved in advance by the manager in the time-tracking tool, except in emergencies (for example a production incident), where it must be recorded within 24 hours.

## Overtime Rates

Overtime is paid on top of the base hourly rate as follows:

- Hours worked beyond 40 and up to 48 in a week are paid at 125% of the base hourly rate.
- Hours worked beyond 48 in a week are paid at 150% of the base hourly rate.
- Hours worked on a public holiday are paid at 200% of the base hourly rate, regardless of the weekly total. Public holiday hours do not count toward the 40-hour threshold.

## Maximum Working Time

No employee may work more than 48 hours per week on average over a 4-month reference period, and never more than 60 hours in a single week. Employees must have at least 11 consecutive hours of rest between two working days and at least one full day off per week.

## On-call Duty

Engineers on the on-call rotation receive a flat on-call allowance of 150 USD per week of on-call duty. Time actually spent working on incidents while on call is paid as overtime for non-exempt employees, or compensated with time off in lieu for exempt employees.
