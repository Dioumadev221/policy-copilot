# Remote Work Policy

Policy owner: People Operations. Version 2.1, effective 1 January 2025.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for remote work once they have completed their probation period. Remote work is not available for roles that require physical presence, which are: lab technician, hardware test engineer, facilities, and reception.

Eligibility also requires a most recent performance rating of "meets expectations" or higher. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible until the plan is successfully completed.

## Number of Remote Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Employees in roles designated as remote-first (software engineering, data, design, and customer support) may work remotely up to 5 days per week, but must attend the quarterly on-site planning week.

## Working From Abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, subject to manager approval and a tax and immigration check by People Operations at least 15 working days before departure. Working from abroad is never allowed from countries under international sanctions.

## Equipment and Allowance

The company provides a laptop, a monitor, and a headset. Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 500 USD and a monthly connectivity allowance of 40 USD.

## Security Requirements

Remote work must be done over a secured network. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN enabled. Confidential documents must not be printed at home. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT security within 2 hours.
