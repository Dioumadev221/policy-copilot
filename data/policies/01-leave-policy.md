# Leave Policy

Policy owner: People Operations. Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2025. Applies to all employees of Helios Robotics.

## Annual Leave

### Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive the same entitlement prorated by their FTE (full-time equivalent). For example, an employee working at 0.6 FTE receives 15 days per year.

### Seniority bonus

Employees receive one additional day of annual leave for every 3 full years of continuous service, up to a maximum of 3 additional days. The bonus is applied from the first full month after the service anniversary.

### Accrual

Annual leave accrues monthly. One twelfth of the yearly entitlement (including any seniority bonus) is credited at the end of each completed calendar month of employment. Employees who join mid-month start accruing from the first day of the following month. Balances are calculated in days and rounded to two decimals.

### Carryover

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March, after which they expire. Any unused days above the 5-day cap are forfeited on 31 December and are not paid out, except where local law requires it.

### Requesting leave

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences of 5 days or more, and at least 2 working days in advance for shorter absences. Managers must answer within 3 working days. Requests that overlap with a team blackout period (for example a product launch week) may be declined.

## Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work because of illness must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Up to 3 consecutive days of sick leave may be self-certified. From the 4th consecutive day, a medical certificate is required and must be uploaded to the HR portal within 48 hours.

Helios Robotics pays 100% of base salary for the first 30 days of sick leave in any rolling 12-month period, and 80% from day 31 to day 90. Beyond 90 days, statutory sick pay applies.

## Parental Leave

### Primary caregiver

The primary caregiver is entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can start up to 2 weeks before the expected due date or placement date of a child.

### Secondary caregiver

The secondary caregiver is entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. It can be taken in up to 3 separate blocks of at least one week each.

### Eligibility

Parental leave is available to all employees who have completed their probation period. Employees still on probation receive the statutory minimum only.

## Bereavement and Special Leave

Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of a spouse, partner, child, or parent, and up to 2 paid days for other close relatives (siblings, grandparents, in-laws). One paid day is granted for moving house once per calendar year. Jury duty and civic obligations are paid in full for their duration.
